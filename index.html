Our story

It started with my own appointment in Ginza.

I came to Tokyo as a visitor and booked Art Makeup at a small Ginza clinic. I expected to feel lost — instead the doctor explained everything in careful English, the room was immaculate, and the result was beautifully natural. No upsell, no markup, just real care.

I kept thinking: other visitors deserve to find this without the stress of guessing which clinic to trust. So I built Kiyora Tokyo — to introduce travelers to the clinics I'd send my own friends to, for free.

Before you book — fees & cancellation
Kiyora's service is free. The fees below are paid directly to the clinic, not to Kiyora.
Cancellation (per treatment)
  • Free until 3 days before
  • ¥5,500 from 2 days before until 12:00 the previous day
  • ¥11,000 from 12:00 to 19:00 the previous day
  • ¥22,000 on the day, or for no-shows
  • Free if you cancel within 12 hours of booking
Rescheduling to another date also incurs these fees.
Time change on the day
¥3,300 for first-time patients (moving your time by an hour or more).
Other fees
  • First-visit fee ¥3,630 (when a treatment is performed the same day)
  • ¥11,000 if you book a consultation only, without treatment
  • Optional fee to request the clinic director (varies by treatment)
  • Some treatments carry a 20% Saturday surcharge
Travel plans change — tell us as early as you can and we'll handle it with the clinic.
