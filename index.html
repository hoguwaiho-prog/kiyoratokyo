Our story

It started with my own appointment in Ginza.

I came to Tokyo as a visitor and booked Art Makeup at a small Ginza clinic. I expected to feel lost — instead the doctor explained everything in careful English, the room was immaculate, and the result was beautifully natural. No upsell, no markup, just real care.

I kept thinking: other visitors deserve to find this without the stress of guessing which clinic to trust. So I built Kiyora Tokyo — to introduce travelers to the clinics I'd send my own friends to, for free.
