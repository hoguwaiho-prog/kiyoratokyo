A practical guide for travelers seeking trusted aesthetic treatments — Botox, fillers, art makeup, HIFU, and laser — in Ginza, Azabu, and central Tokyo.

Updated May 2026 · 8 min read · By Kiyora Tokyo Editorial

Why Tokyo for beauty treatments?

Japan's medical tourism market reached $5.98 billion in 2025 and continues to grow at 22% annually. While Korea dominates surgical procedures, Tokyo has quietly become the leading destination for non-surgical, minimally invasive treatments — the kind that fit perfectly into a 5-day trip.

What makes Tokyo different:

✅ Minimal-downtime treatments dominate (Botox, fillers, HIFU)

✅ Strict medical regulations — every procedure performed by licensed physicians

✅ Transparent pricing — most clinics publish prices online

✅ No "tourist markup" — you pay the same price as locals

How we selected these clinics

We applied 5 strict criteria. Every clinic listed must have English-speaking staff (verified by direct contact), be located in Ginza or Minato-ku (accessible from major hotels), specialize in non-surgical aesthetic procedures, offer same-day or next-day availability for travelers, and maintain a transparent pricing structure with no hidden fees.

We excluded large chain clinics that prioritize volume over personalized care.

What to Expect at a Japanese Beauty Clinic

Consultation first. Every clinic requires a consultation — usually 20–30 minutes — before any treatment. This is included in the price at most clinics listed above.

Conservative approach. Japanese aesthetic medicine prioritizes natural results. Don't expect aggressive Korean-style transformations. If subtle is what you want, you're in the right place.

Strict medical standards. All treatments must be performed by licensed physicians (not nurses or estheticians, as is common elsewhere). This means slightly higher prices but significantly safer outcomes.

Payment. Most clinics accept Visa, Mastercard, and JCB. Some require cash for treatments under ¥30,000.

Common Questions from Travelers

Will my treatment results be visible during my trip?

Most non-surgical treatments show results within 3–14 days. Botox typically takes 5–7 days for full effect. HIFU and laser results develop over 4–6 weeks.

Can I fly home immediately after treatment?

Yes for Botox, fillers, and most laser treatments. Wait 24 hours after HIFU. Avoid flying for 48 hours after thread lifts.

Are prices negotiable?

No. Japanese clinics use fixed pricing. The published price is what you pay.

Do I need health insurance?

No. Aesthetic treatments are not covered by Japanese health insurance for tourists. All payments are out-of-pocket.

What about language barriers in emergencies?

All clinics listed have at least one English-speaking staff member during business hours. For after-hours support, Kiyora Tokyo provides English support to clients we match.
